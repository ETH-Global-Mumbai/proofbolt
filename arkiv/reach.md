# Reach

> Draft: the team should correct this before submitting, especially the last section.

## Who it is for

People who hand out temporary physical access without being there, such as shared lockers, parcel handoff, and equipment rooms. They need a person to prove they are present, access that ends by itself, and a record they don't have to take on trust.

## Would they use it

This is a hackathon prototype, and we haven't tested it with outside users. The status page is what they would use first, since anyone can open it to check a locker with no account.

## Can they try it today

Anyone can read the live data with the query in the [README](../README.md#check-it-yourself). Running a locker takes this repo, an ESP8266 and the setup steps in the README. It runs on testnets, and the World ID check is currently a stand-in.
